How to get support for Avon.

## Course and teaching questions

Start with your **course team** or module convenor. They set how Avon is used on your course (repos, deadlines, feedback).

## Platform and registration

For tool registration, keys, and grade passback on your VLE, contact your **institution’s platform / VLE admin**, or the Avon operator for your deployment.

## Product and deployment

If you run Avon for your organisation, use the support channel your Avon contact provided for that environment.

## These docs

Something missing or unclear in the docs? Tell your Avon contact so we can improve the page.
