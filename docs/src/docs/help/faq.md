Short answers to questions course teams and admins often ask.

## Do students need an Avon account?

No. Students open Avon from the course activity in the VLE. There is no separate Avon password for that path.

## How do staff place an activity?

Register Avon as an LTI 1.3 external tool, then use **Select content** (or your platform’s equivalent) to pick an activity from Avon’s catalog. See [Learning platforms](/integrate/lms).

## Can grades go back to the VLE?

Yes, when the platform supports LTI Assignment and Grade Services and the tool is configured for grade passback. See [Grades & assessment](/product/assess).

## Where does student code live?

In private repositories on your institution’s Git host. [Provisioning](/product/provision) creates student or team repos from a template; students still launch work from the VLE.

## Launch URL expired or bookmark failed

Open the activity again from the course page. Mid-flow launch links are short-lived on purpose.
