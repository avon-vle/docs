Avon helps computer science courses run the full loop of **set up student repos → give feedback → mark work** without bouncing between the VLE, a forge, and half a dozen tabs.

Students open Avon from Moodle, Canvas, or another LTI 1.3 learning platform - the same place they already find the rest of the module. Lecturers work from those launches too, with a staff view instead of a student one.

These docs are for people **using** Avon on a course: how it fits a module, what students see, and how to connect your learning platform.

## Who this is for

- **Lecturers and course teams** - how Avon fits a module, what students see, and how each product area works.
- **Students** - what Avon is for in plain terms (your lecturer will say when to use it).
- **Platform / VLE admins** - adding Avon as an external tool and how launches behave (see **Platforms**).

## What Avon does

Four connected jobs make up the product:

| Job                     | What it does                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Provisioning repos**  | One template becomes private repos for every student or team |
| **Testing & feedback**  | Autograder results next to the work, not buried in CI        |
| **Review suggestions**  | Hints and review help tied to _this_ submission              |
| **Grades & assessment** | Marks and structure that go back to the VLE                  |

Staff pick an activity in the course, students and instructors launch into the right workspace, and the session stays tied to the course tool - not a separate Avon login.

## How people open Avon

1. **Public website and these docs** - no course session needed.
2. **Inside the VLE** - staff add an Avon activity; everyone launches it from the course page.

You do not need a separate Avon password for that course path. After you click the activity, Avon checks the launch with the platform, then opens a session in the browser for instructor or learner work.

## Getting started

On a deployment where Avon is registered with your VLE:

1. Add Avon as an LTI 1.3 external tool ([Learning platforms](/integrate/lms), [LTI setup](/integrate/lti)).
2. In a course, use **Select content** (or your platform’s equivalent) and pick an Avon activity.
3. Launch it - teaching staff get the instructor workspace; students get the learner workspace.
