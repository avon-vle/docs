**Review suggestions** is feedback help that stays **tied to a specific submission** - follow-up questions, scoped hints, and review of the diff that matters - rather than a generic chatbot in another tab.

## What course teams get

- Look at the **change this student made**, not a free-floating chat.
- Draft follow-ups or comments that match the coursework brief.
- Keep assisted output something staff can inspect and stay accountable for.

Students experience help as part of **this** piece of work, not a separate product login.

## How it fits review

Suggestions stay scoped to the submission and activity. Staff can use them while reviewing, then edit or reject anything before it becomes official feedback.

Course policy still applies: what models are allowed, how assisted feedback is disclosed, and academic integrity rules for your institution.
