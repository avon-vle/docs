**Grades & assessment** is where coursework outcomes become marks in the learning platform: who has submitted, structured review, and sending scores back to the VLE.

## What course teams get

- See who has submitted and what still needs attention.
- Mark with structure (not only a parallel spreadsheet).
- Push scores back to Moodle, Canvas, or other platforms that support LTI grade services.

Students see assessment as part of the same activity they already open from the course - not a second grading portal.

## Graded activities

When you select content from the VLE, you can place graded activities such as a **graded project checkpoint** (with a max score and line item when the platform allows it).

Avon uses **Assignment and Grade Services (AGS)** where the platform advertises them - the standard LTI path for scores and line items.

If your platform does not accept line items for the tool, graded options that need them may not appear. That is a platform capability issue, not something students can fix from their side.

## Working with the gradebook

- Confirm with your platform admin that grade passback is enabled for the Avon tool.
- Use a staff account to place graded content; use a student account to confirm what learners can and cannot see.
- Instructor workspace is where cohort review and marking sit; learner workspace is where students see their own outcomes.
